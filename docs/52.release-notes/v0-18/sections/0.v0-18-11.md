## 🚀 Improvements

### 🧭 Console Navigation Reorganization

We've redesigned the console's navigation to make it easier and faster to access the tools you need. Key actions are now available from anywhere in the console, reducing clicks and improving your workflow.

Essential actions have moved to a persistent top-right menu bar that remains accessible regardless of which screen you're viewing:
- **Quick Access Items**: 
  - Dashboard – View live status of running machines and tasks at a glance;
  - Achievements – Track your milestones and earned achievements;
  - Current Balance & Buy Credits – Monitor your credit balance and top up instantly;
  - Notifications – Stay updated with real-time alerts about your jobs and system events;
  - Discord – Direct link to Inductiva's [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/rFkHxVmAbu) for community support and real-time assistance.

![top_right](releases/v0-18/top_right.png)

- **Account Menu** - Access account-related features through the account icon dropdown:
  - Manage Profile (NEW - see below) 
  - Top-ups – View your credit purchase history;
  - Costs – Review monthly spending broken down by compute, storage, data transfer, and **Inductiva Orchestration Fee**.

![profile](releases/v0-18/profile.png)

These changes reflect our commitment to making the console more intuitive and efficient for your computational workflows.

### 🪪 Revamped Profile screen

We’ve completely redesigned the Profile screen to give users clearer visibility into their account details and to support new platform capabilities.

![profile_screen](releases/v0-18/profile_screen.png)

What’s new:

* **Centralized Account Overview**  
All essential information about your account is now in one place. You can quickly see:  
– Your price plan;  
– Your usage quotas;
– Any applicable fees;  
– The organization you belong to (if any).  
Previously scattered across different areas, this data is now consolidated for full transparency.

* **Location Settings for Better Performance**  
You can now set your country and region directly in your profile.  
Keeping this information up to date ensures more efficient simulations: the platform will soon automatically select the nearest datacenter by default, reducing latency and improving performance, so you can already get ahead by updating your location information now!

* **Discord Handle for Faster Support**  
You can now add your Discord handle. This allows us to match your messages with your Inductiva account, speeding up support and improving issue resolution quality.  

### 📊 System Metrics: Now with GPU Monitoring

The [System Metrics](/guides/machine_simulation/system-metrics) on the Console, available under Task Details → Runtime subtab, now shows **real-time GPU utilization** and **VRAM usage** when a GPU is active, complementing the existing CPU and RAM metrics.

These updates make it easier to monitor your system resources, optimize simulations, and diagnose performance issues.

## 📚 Documentation & Resources

**New SWASH FAQ Entry: Logs Behavior Clarification**  
  We’ve added a new entry to the SWASH FAQ regarding log generation when tasks fail on Inductiva’s cloud machines:  

  - On **local machines** (macOS or Windows), SWASH failures still produce output files like `errfile` or `print`.  
  - On **Inductiva**, these files may occasionally be missing due to the Fortran parameter `gfortran_unbuffered_all`, which controls unbuffered file writing:  
    - **Local machines:** `True` → logs always written  
    - **Inductiva cloud machines:** `False` → best performance, but logs may not appear after a failed task  

  You can choose the behavior that best fits your workflow:  
  - Keep default (`False`) for optimal performance (occasional missing logs expected)  
  - Set to `True` to ensure logs are always written (may reduce performance for disk-intensive simulations)  

  See the new SWASH FAQ entry for guidance on `gfortran_unbuffered_all` [here](/guides/swash/faq/faq).
